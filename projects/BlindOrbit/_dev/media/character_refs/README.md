## 1. 林子修 (Lin Tzu-Hsiu) — Codename: Skywatcher

### 定裝照 (Character Sheet / Turnaround)

```
Photorealistic character turnaround sheet of a Taiwanese Air Force Major in his early 30s. Front view, three-quarter view, and side profile arranged on a neutral dark grey backdrop. Lean athletic build, short-cropped black hair with a clean military cut, sharp cheekbones, intense dark brown eyes with a distant thousand-yard quality. Wearing an olive-green Nomex flight suit with Taiwanese ROCAF patches on the shoulder, zipped to the chest, a g-suit harness loosely hanging at the waist. Dog tags visible at the collar. Skin has a slight sheen from humidity. Hands are slender, precise — a pilot's hands. Expression is controlled, jaw slightly clenched. Inspired by a young Tony Leung Chiu-wai circa In the Mood for Love but leaner and more militarized. Studio lighting with a single cool-white key light from upper left creating defined shadows on the jawline, subtle blue rim light on the right edge. Shot on ARRI Alexa 65, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field. Color palette: olive, gunmetal grey, muted teal. 8K resolution, hyperdetailed skin texture.
```

### 場景內 (In-Context Shot)

```
Cinematic wide shot of a Taiwanese Air Force Major seated alone in a dimly lit Cold War-era radar operations center. Dozens of green CRT screens cast pale emerald light across his face. He leans forward, both hands flat on the metal console, staring at a radar sweep showing unidentified contacts approaching from the west. The room is cramped, filled with analog dials, oscilloscopes, and bundled cables. Other stations are empty — he is the last one on watch. A single overhead fluorescent tube flickers intermittently. His flight suit is unzipped to mid-chest, sweat visible at the collar. A half-empty paper cup of cold tea sits beside his right hand. The mood is submarine-film claustrophobia meets Wong Kar-wai melancholy. Camera angle: low, slightly Dutch, shooting past a rack of equipment in the foreground. Lighting: practical green CRT glow as primary, cold fluorescent overhead as fill, deep shadows everywhere else. Film grain, anamorphic lens flare. Color palette: deep green, black, steel blue. Reference: Das Boot meets Infernal Affairs. Aspect ratio 2.39:1 cinemascope.

### 情緒特寫 (Emotional Close-Up)

```

His left hand is raised near his ear but not touching it — fingers trembling almost imperceptibly. Eyes are glassy, pupils slightly dilated, staring past the camera into nothing. A single bead of sweat traces down his temple. His jaw muscles are visibly flexed, lips pressed into a thin line — the portrait of a man containing an invisible scream. The background is a complete blur of green radar light. Shallow depth of field, only the plane of his eyes in sharp focus. Lighting: cold green from below (reflected CRT light), warm amber from a distant doorway behind him creating a thin halo on his hair. Micro skin detail — pores, fine scars near the hairline. The aesthetic of a Wong Kar-wai close-up shot by Christopher Doyle. Film stock texture, 35mm grain. Color palette: jade green, amber, shadow black.

Behind him, completely out of focus, the amber glow of a dust storm. One bloodshot vein is visible in the white of his left eye. The portrait of chemical erasure as a survival strategy. Lighting: warm amber ambient from the dust storm, a single shaft of direct sunlight hitting just the bridge of his nose and one eye, leaving the other in shadow. Extreme skin detail — sun damage, peeling skin on the nose, fine dust in every pore. Shot on Panavision Millennium DXL2 with a vintage C-series anamorphic, wide open for maximum optical character. Color palette: amber, dust, pale skin, bloodshot pink. Reference: the final shot of No Country for Old Men — a face accepting entropy.

## 使用說明 (Usage Notes)

### Midjourney 參數建議

在 prompt 末尾加入以下參數：

- `--ar 16:9` 或 `--ar 21:9`（場景內 / 情緒特寫）
- `--ar 3:4`（定裝照）
- `--s 250`（高風格化）
- `--q 2`（高品質）
- `--v 6.1` 或更新版本

### Flux / Veo 提示建議

- 直接使用完整 prompt 文字
- 若生成結果不理想，可將 prompt 拆分為「主體描述」+「環境描述」+「攝影技術描述」三段分別強化
- 加入 negative prompt：`cartoon, anime, illustration, 3D render, plastic skin, oversaturated, stock photo`

### 角色一致性維護

若需跨場景維持角色一致性：

1. 先以「定裝照 prompt」生成基準圖像
2. 使用該圖像作為 character reference（Midjourney `--cref`）
3. 場景內與情緒特寫 prompt 搭配 `--cref [基準圖 URL] --cw 100` 使用
4. 在 Flux 中使用 IP-Adapter 或 InstantID 以基準圖像鎖定臉部特徵

### 風格統一關鍵詞

所有 prompt 共享的基底風格詞，可額外附加：

```
photorealistic, cinematic, 35mm film grain, anamorphic lens, natural skin texture, atmospheric haze, muted desaturated color grade, editorial photography, Roger Deakins cinematography style
```
