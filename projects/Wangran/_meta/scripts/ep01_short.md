*   **配音設定**：男主第一人稱旁白 (VO)。聲音低沉、帶有壓抑的沙啞。
*   **音樂設定**：開場僅有孤獨的滴答聲，中段帶出懸疑冷硬的低音，結尾爆發悲傷的弦樂。

> `Generate a vertical 9:16 aspect ratio image. Extreme close-up shot of a vintage pocket watch under a warm desk lamp, the hands are stuck exactly at 3:07. A man's fingers, slightly stained with watchmaker oil, are adjusting the tiny gears. Photorealistic cinematic film still, shot on 35mm lens, highly detailed. Cold blue and grey cinematic environment with warm tungsten lighting contrast, moody lighting. Do NOT make it look like an illustration.`
