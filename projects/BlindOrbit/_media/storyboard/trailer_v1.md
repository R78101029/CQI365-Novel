# Blind Orbit (盲軌：2028) - Cinematic Trailer Storyboard v1

> **Duration**: ~90 seconds
> **Aspect Ratio**: 2.39:1 (Anamorphic Widescreen)
> **Color Palette**: Cold steel blue / amber warning orange / deep black
> **Tagline**: 「當世界的眼睛被蒙上，你只能相信直覺。」(When the world goes blind, trust only your instincts.)

## Shot 01 — The Connected World

**Duration**: 8 seconds

### Scene Description
Dawn breaks over the Pacific Ocean. We see the curvature of Earth from low orbit — a lattice of communication satellites drifts serenely across the frame, their solar panels catching golden sunlight. Faint data-stream lines (visualized as thin blue threads) connect the satellites to ground stations below. The ocean surface glimmers. Everything is orderly, pristine, interconnected.

### Camera Movement & Angle
Slow orbital dolly shot, camera drifting laterally alongside a cluster of military satellites. Gentle parallax against the Earth's surface below. Camera tilts slightly downward to reveal the Pacific, Taiwan's coastline barely visible through wisps of cloud.

### Veo 3.1 Prompt
```
Cinematic orbital shot of Earth from low orbit at dawn. A constellation of military communication satellites with golden solar panels drift through frame in the foreground. Thin luminous blue data-link lines connect the satellites to ground stations on Earth's surface below. The Pacific Ocean glimmers with early morning light. Taiwan's green coastline is faintly visible through scattered clouds. Anamorphic lens flare from the rising sun. Photorealistic, 2.39:1 aspect ratio, shot on ARRI Alexa 65. Serene, calm, beautiful. No text or UI overlays.
```

### Audio Notes
- **Music**: Single sustained piano note (C minor), reverb-heavy. Faint orchestral pad underneath.
- **SFX**: Gentle electronic hum of satellite telemetry. Soft static crackle.
- **Voiceover (V.O.)**:
  - ZH: 「二零二八年十一月十日。世界仍在呼吸。」
  - EN: *"November 10th, 2028. The world still breathes."*

## Shot 02 — The Calm Before

**Duration**: 7 seconds

### Scene Description
Interior of the Leshan (樂山) radar station, nestled in the mountains of central Taiwan. Dim green glow of CRT and LCD monitors. A PAVE PAWS AN/FPS-115 phased-array radar display shows clean sweeps — no contacts. Technicians sit relaxed at their stations. **Lin Tzu-Hsiu** (mid-30s, lean, military buzz cut, Major's insignia on his flight suit) stands at the back of the operations room, arms crossed, staring at the screen. His expression is calm but alert — the faintest tension around his eyes. A coffee mug sits untouched beside a keyboard.

### Camera Movement & Angle
Slow push-in through the radar room. Camera starts wide (establishing the space, the operators, the green glow) and gradually narrows to a medium close-up on Lin Tzu-Hsiu's face. Shallow depth of field isolates him from the background.

## Shot 03 — Sea of Tranquility

**Duration**: 6 seconds

### Scene Description
Same radar room — but everything has changed. Every screen simultaneously erupts into white noise and cascading error codes. The clean radar sweep is replaced by a blizzard of phantom contacts — hundreds of false targets flooding the display. Red warning lights strobe across the ceiling. Technicians leap to their feet. Lin Tzu-Hsiu's coffee mug vibrates off the desk and shatters on the floor. His eyes widen — not with surprise, but with recognition. He grabs the analog radio handset.

### Camera Movement & Angle
Crash zoom from the radar screen (showing clean sweep transforming into chaos) pulling back rapidly to a wide shot of the entire operations room plunging into alarm state. Handheld camera shake. Strobing red light creates staccato illumination.

  - ZH: (whispered, almost lost in the noise) 「寧靜海。」
  - EN: *"Sea of Tranquility."*

## Shot 04 — Global Blackout

**Duration**: 8 seconds

### Scene Description
A rapid montage sequence (3 locations, ~2.7s each) showing the cascade of blindness across the globe:

**(A) — The Pentagon, Washington D.C.** General Adler stands before a massive wall of screens in the National Military Command Center. One by one, every screen goes dark — satellite feeds, force tracker displays, communication channels. A four-star general watches his entire military go blind. An aide hands him a red telephone receiver — the last working line.

**(B) — NATO HQ, Brussels.** A crowded situation room. Officers stare at blank screens. Sophie Laurent (blonde, tailored suit, mid-40s) stands calmly while everyone else panics — she already knew this was coming. A subtle smirk crosses her lips.

**(C) — Low Earth Orbit.** A US military SBIRS satellite drifts silently. Its solar panels are intact, its body undamaged — but its signal indicator light flickers from green to red. The data-stream threads from Shot 01 snap and dissolve like severed nerves. The satellite is alive but lobotomized.

### Camera Movement & Angle
(A) Steadicam tracking shot around Adler as screens die. (B) Slow push-in on Sophie's face — isolating her calm amid chaos. (C) Static wide shot of the satellite, camera perfectly still as the data threads disintegrate.

## Shot 05 — The Assault Begins

**Duration**: 8 seconds

### Scene Description
Exterior. Pre-dawn darkness over the Taiwan Strait. Shot from sea level, looking east toward Taiwan's coast. The horizon is ink-black — then suddenly, a swarm of attack drones (Sea Hag / 海巫) rises from behind a line of silhouetted warships. They are triangular, stealthy, matte-black — dozens of them lifting off in perfect formation like a flock of mechanical ravens. Their engine glow is a dull red. Behind them, the flashes of cruise missile launches illuminate the warships in staccato pulses. The scale is overwhelming — this is not a surgical strike, this is an invasion.

### Camera Movement & Angle
Low-angle shot from the waterline, looking up at the drones as they pass overhead. Camera slowly tilts upward to follow the swarm. Wide anamorphic frame emphasizes the sheer number. Lens catches water spray in the foreground.

Ko shouts orders to his infantry squad through the downpour. Lin clutches a waterproof case to his chest — inside is the encrypted hard drive containing the Link-16 parameters. His hands tremble slightly. An explosion illuminates the ridgeline behind them. Lin looks down at a water-damaged paper map spread across the hood, plotting coordinates by flashlight. No GPS. No data link. Just a man, a map, and the rain.

Handheld, close and claustrophobic. Over-the-shoulder on Lin, then whip-pan to Ko shouting, then back to Lin's trembling hands on the map. Rain hits the lens. Shallow focus racks between Lin's face and the distant explosion.

## Production Notes

### Visual Language
- **Pre-attack**: Clean compositions, steady camera, warm light. Order and control.
- **Post-attack**: Handheld, tight framing, desaturated palette. Chaos and isolation.
- **Reconnection**: Return to steady camera but with harder, battle-worn lighting. Not restored to the original beauty — scarred but functional.

### Color Grading Reference
| Act | Primary | Secondary | Feel |
|-----|---------|-----------|------|
| Peace (1-2) | Steel blue | Gold/amber | Serene, clinical |
| Strike (3-5) | Red strobe / white static | Black | Alarm, disorientation |
| Chaos (6-8) | Desaturated green (TW), Blue-silver (EU), Bleached white (ME) | Orange fire | Theater-specific palettes |
| Betrayal (9) | Cold blue split with warm amber | Sickly green | Paranoia |
| Hope (10-11) | Amber rebuilding to blue-gold | Clean white | Cautious resolve |
| Title (12) | Pure black + cold blue text | None | Finality |

### Voiceover Direction
- Speaker: Male, 40s, deep baritone. Taiwanese Mandarin accent (not Beijing standard).
- Tone: Controlled, measured. Not dramatic — almost detached, like a military briefing that gradually cracks with emotion.
- Pacing: Slow and deliberate in Peace. Clipped and urgent in Strike/Chaos. Quiet and reflective in Hope.

### Music Reference
- Hans Zimmer's *Dunkirk* (ticking tension)
- Jóhann Jóhannsson's *Arrival* (alien dread, subsonic presence)
- Ryuichi Sakamoto's *The Revenant* (sparse, organic, wounded beauty)

### Sound Design Reference
- The electronic "death" of satellite systems should feel biological — like hearing a heartbeat flatline
- Radar pings should evolve: clean (Peace) > drowned in static (Strike) > fighting through noise (Chaos) > clear again (Hope)
- Each theater has a signature ambient sound: rain (Taiwan), wind in pines (Europe), sand hiss (Middle East)
