# 《溺墨》插圖規劃與 Prompt 清單 (Image Prompts)

本文件定義《溺墨》(Not Waving but Drowning) 各章節的視覺風格與 AI 生成 Prompt。

## 視覺風格指南 (Visual Style Guide)
- **色調**：墨色、深藍、雨夜灰色、螢幕冷光。
- **元素**：雨水、滴落的墨汁、筆電、反射的鏡面、重疊的人影。
- **氛圍**：壓抑、沉靜、無限嵌套、虛無感、Noir 風格。

---

## 章節 Prompt 清單

### 01｜墨起 (Ink Rising)
- **視覺重點**：基隆雨夜、舊筆電、第一抹墨漬。
- **Prompt**: A writer's desk in Keelung during a rainy night. A single lamp illuminates an old laptop. Tiny black ink stains are appearing on the keyboard. Through the window, heavy rain and blurred city lights are visible. Cinematic, Noir style, moody lighting.
- **預定檔名**: `illus_ch01_ink_rising.png`

### 02｜墨影 (Ink Shadow)
- **視覺重點**：書房門外的影子、墨水滲透門縫。
- **Prompt**: A blurry silhouette of a person standing outside a closed wooden study door. Dark black ink is slowly seeping from under the door and around the door handle. Claustrophobic atmosphere, psychological horror, high contrast.
- **預定檔名**: `illus_ch02_ink_shadow.png`

### 03｜墨雨 (Ink Rain)
- **視覺重點**：天花板滴落的墨水、打字的手。
- **Prompt**: Thick black ink dripping from a cracked ceiling onto a pair of hands typing intensely on a keyboard. The ink spreads across the fingers like a dark web. Rain streaks on the wall behind. Visceral, surreal, intense focus.
- **預定檔名**: `illus_ch03_ink_rain.png`

### 04｜墨鏡 (Ink Mirror)
- **視覺重點**：螢幕反射、身分重疊、延遲的影像。
- **Prompt**: A man staring at a laptop screen. In the reflection of the glass, his face looks exactly like a desperate character from a book, but the reflection is slightly delayed. Surrealism, glitch effect, overlapping identities, blue and dark tones.
- **預定檔名**: `illus_ch04_ink_mirror.png`

### 05｜墨沉 (Ink Submersion)
- **視覺重點**：被墨水淹沒的房間、持續寫作。
- **Prompt**: A study room slowly filling with black ink. A man is sitting at his desk, half-submerged in the dark liquid, but he continues to type on his glowing laptop. The screen is the only light source in the drowning room. Dark, heavy, hopeless.
- **預定檔名**: `illus_ch05_ink_submersion.png`

### 06｜墨空 (Ink Void)
- **視覺重點**：消失的文字、透明的手。
- **Prompt**: A laptop screen that has turned completely black, with the faint, ghostly word 'HOLLOW' visible in the center. The writer's hands are becoming transparent as they fade into the darkness. Existential dread, minimalist, void, cold light.
- **預定檔名**: `illus_ch06_ink_void.png`

### 07｜墨沒 (Ink Disappearance)
- **視覺重點**：乾淨的白室、唯一的門。
- **Prompt**: A clean, minimalist white room with a single closed door at the end of a corridor. No ink, no rain, just blindingly clear morning light. A sense of final, cold clarity. High key, peaceful but terrifyingly empty.
- **預定檔名**: `illus_ch07_ink_disappearance.png`
