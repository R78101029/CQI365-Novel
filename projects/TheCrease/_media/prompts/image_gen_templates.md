## 模板一：故事封面 (Book Cover)

> **設計概念**：展現高維度「摺痕」與物理時間的交錯，渾天儀作為貫穿千年的核心物理錨點。

### 主視覺：渾天儀與時空裂痕
```
A cinematic, photorealistic book cover for a hard sci-fi novel.
A dark, moody, and epic composition.
An ancient bronze Chinese armillary sphere (渾天儀) suspended in the center.
Through its bronze rings, a sharp, glowing, reality-bending fractal tear
or "crease" in space-time is visible, emitting a chaotic mix of cold blue
and amber light. Fading mathematical equations and faint glowing coordinates
are subtly etched into the deep shadows of the background.

Atmosphere: monumental, existential, melancholic, reminiscent of Interstellar
and Arrival. Photographic quality, dramatic lighting, anamorphic lens,
8k resolution, minimalist but visceral sci-fi aesthetic.

--ar 2:3 --style raw --v 6.1
```
