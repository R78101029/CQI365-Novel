## 一、旁白配音 (Voiceover — ElevenLabs / Azure TTS)

### 預告片旁白 — 敘事者聲音描述 (Narrator Voice)

```
Voice: Deep male baritone, Mandarin Chinese (Traditional), age 50-60.
Tone: Gravelly, authoritative, like a war correspondent reflecting
on events he personally witnessed.
Pace: Slow and deliberate, with strategic pauses between sentences.
Each word carries weight. Never rushed.
Emotion: Restrained intensity — not dramatic, but weighted with the
knowledge of what's coming. The calm of someone who has already
seen the worst.
Breathing: Audible between phrases — not theatrical, just human.
Reference: documentary narrator meets military briefing officer.
Think Morgan Freeman's gravitas but in Mandarin.
```

### 角色聲音描述 (Character Voice Profiles)

#### 林子修 (Skywatcher)

```
Voice: Young male tenor, Mandarin Chinese (Taiwan accent), age 30-34.
Tone: Clipped, precise, military radio protocol cadence. Speaks like
someone accustomed to giving technical reports over noisy comm channels.
Pace: Fast when relaying data, slower when making critical decisions.
Emotion: Controlled calm that barely contains urgency. You can hear the
effort it takes to keep his voice steady.
Characteristic: Occasionally pauses mid-sentence — a sharp intake of
breath — representing tinnitus episodes. Never acknowledges it aloud.
Vocabulary: Technical jargon mixed with Taiwanese military slang.
Short declarative sentences. Rarely uses "I feel" — prefers "I assess."
Sample line: 「雷達幕上有異常波形。不是雜訊——是人為的。啟動勝利女神。」
```

#### 伊萊亞斯·沃格爾 (Jaeger)

```
Voice: Male baritone, German-accented English, age 40-42.
Tone: Low, gravelly, speaks through clenched teeth as if every word
costs him physically. The voice of chronic pain being ignored.
Pace: Measured, deliberate. Never raises his voice — reduces volume
for emphasis instead.
Emotion: Sardonic exhaustion. Dry, bitter humor deployed in the
darkest moments as a coping mechanism.
Characteristic: Occasionally mutters in German before translating to
English. Clears his throat frequently (stomach ulcer side effect).
Vocabulary: Intelligence analyst precision. Favors passive voice
("It has been confirmed" over "I confirmed"). Formal even in chaos.
Sample line: "The codebook says TROJAN. Which means Brussels has
been compromised for... longer than I care to calculate."
```

#### 凱恩 (Nomad)

```
Voice: Male, flat American Midwest accent, age 36-38.
Tone: Monotone, minimal inflection, economy of words. Like a machine
that learned to speak and decided most words weren't worth the effort.
Pace: Sparse. Responds after long pauses. Sometimes doesn't respond at all.
Emotion: Complete emotional flatness — clinical detachment that might
be nihilism or might be brain damage.
Characteristic: Never uses more words than necessary. Long silences
between responses. When he does speak, it's either tactical information
or something unexpectedly blunt about the human condition.
Vocabulary: Military brevity codes. Single-word answers. No small talk.
Sample line: "Contact. Three tangos. Two hundred meters. Moving."
(pause) "Handled."
```

#### 柯大勇 (Captain Ko — Black Bear)

```
Voice: Male, rough baritone, Mandarin (Southern Taiwan accent), age 34-36.
Tone: Blunt, commanding, no-nonsense field officer. The voice of a man
who has been shouting orders over gunfire for years.
Pace: Rapid-fire in combat, gruff and short in conversation.
Emotion: Protective anger. Cares deeply but expresses it through
aggression and dark humor.
Characteristic: Peppers speech with Taiwanese Hokkien profanity.
Barks orders. Never explains twice.
Vocabulary: Infantry field slang. Direct, vulgar, effective.
Sample line: 「全排注意！那個戴眼鏡的少校比你們任何一個人都重要。他死了，我們全完了。聽懂沒有？」
```

#### 「老師」(The Teacher — 陳維國)

```
Voice: Older male, Mandarin (Beijing accent), age 60s.
Tone: Soft, scholarly, almost gentle — a professor lecturing to his
brightest students. The calm is what makes him terrifying.
Pace: Measured, unhurried. Never raised, never strained. He has all
the time in the world because he believes he's already won.
Emotion: Serene confidence bordering on condescension. Genuine belief
in historical inevitability.
Characteristic: Speaks in measured cadences, often uses historical
analogies and chess metaphors. Quotes Sun Tzu without attribution,
as if the thoughts are his own.
Vocabulary: Classical Chinese idioms, strategic terminology, academic
precision. Never uses coarse language.
Sample line: 「美國人的弱點，不是他們的武器——是他們的想像力。他們無法想像有人不想成為他們。」
```

#### 蘇菲·洛朗 (Sophie Laurent)

```
Voice: Female, French-accented English, age 45.
Tone: Cultured, controlled, diplomatic — the carefully modulated voice
of someone who has spent decades in European politics.
Pace: Measured and articulate, accelerates when defensive or cornered.
Emotion: Surface composure masking growing panic. As the story
progresses, the veneer cracks — her voice tightens, her accent
thickens, pauses get longer.
Characteristic: Uses bureaucratic euphemisms to avoid reality.
Says "de-escalation" when she means "surrender." Corrects herself
mid-sentence when she slips.
Sample line: "The Peace Dove directive ensures that our forces
maintain a posture of — of strategic restraint. This is not
appeasement. This is... responsible governance."
```

## 二、配樂 (Music — Suno / Udio)

### 預告片配樂結構 (Trailer Score Structure)

#### Phase 1: Peace (0:00-0:15) — 寧靜
```
Prompt for Suno/Udio:

Ambient orchestral, single piano note with cathedral reverb,
distant string pad, very sparse arrangement. Like Ryuichi Sakamoto
"Merry Christmas Mr Lawrence" meets Max Richter "On the Nature of
Daylight". Tempo: 60 BPM. Key: D minor.

Instruments: solo grand piano (Steinway, close-mic'd), ambient
synth pads, distant solo cello with vibrato.

Mood: beautiful but fragile, like glass about to shatter. A world
that doesn't know it's about to end. Nostalgic, melancholy,
the last peaceful moment.

Do NOT include drums, percussion, or any rhythmic element.
Pure atmosphere.
```

#### Phase 2: The Strike (0:15-0:37) — 致盲
```
Prompt for Suno/Udio:

Electronic pulse mixed with orchestral tension building. Hans Zimmer
"Dunkirk" ticking clock motif meets Trent Reznor digital anxiety.
Tempo: builds from 80 to 110 BPM over 22 seconds.

Instruments: Shepard tone (ever-rising), staccato string section
(16th notes), deep sub-bass pulses every 4 beats, glitchy digital
artifacts and distorted radio static samples interspersed,
single snare drum building in intensity.

Mood: systemic collapse, technological dread. Something invisible
is destroying everything. The rising tone should create physical
anxiety in the listener.

Build: gradual crescendo with increasing rhythmic density. Each
4-bar phrase adds a new layer. By the end, it should feel like
too much information — sonic overload.
```

#### Phase 3: Chaos (0:37-0:59) — 爆發
```
Prompt for Suno/Udio:

Full orchestral assault meets industrial electronic. Trent Reznor /
Atticus Ross "The Social Network" intensity meets Junkie XL
"Mad Max Fury Road" driving force. Tempo: 130 BPM, relentless.

Instruments: brass section stabs (horns and trombones), taiko drum
ensemble, distorted electric guitar power chords, synthetic bass
drops, rapid-fire snare and kick drum pattern, orchestral strings
doing tremolo runs.

Mood: three simultaneous wars erupting — overwhelming, kinetic,
visceral. The listener should feel like they're being hit from all
sides. Controlled chaos, not random noise.

Structure: 4-bar phrases, each cutting between "Asia texture"
(pentatonic melodic fragments), "Europe texture" (minor key brass),
"Middle East texture" (augmented scale runs).
```

#### Phase 4: Betrayal (0:59-1:07) — 背叛
```
Prompt for Suno/Udio:

Sudden silence — all instruments cut. Two beats of nothing.
Then a single dissonant low brass chord (trombone + tuba + contrabass).
Reference: the "BRAAAM" from Inception but more subtle and more
human. A heartbeat thump underneath. Reverse cymbal swell.

Mood: the knife twist — realization of internal betrayal. The enemy
is inside the house. Everything you thought was safe is compromised.

Drop all instruments to near silence. Just a pulse. Just a heartbeat.
Let the silence do the work.
```

#### Phase 5: Hope (1:07-1:22) — 重建
```
Prompt for Suno/Udio:

Rebuilt from the silence. The piano from Phase 1 returns — same
melody but transposed up a major third. Strings join gradually.
Hans Zimmer "Interstellar" organ grandeur meets Johann Johannsson
"Arrival" alien beauty. Tempo: 90 BPM, steady and purposeful.

Instruments: piano melody (now with more conviction), building
string section (cellos → violas → violins), French horn carrying
a counter-melody, military snare roll underneath, organ pedal tone.

Mood: desperate determination, NOT triumph. This is NOT a victory
theme. This is "we might survive if we fight hard enough."
Earned hope, not given hope. Bittersweet, costly.
```

#### Phase 6: Title (1:22-1:30) — 片名
```
Prompt for Suno/Udio:

All instruments cut abruptly. Single electronic ping — clean,
digital, precise. The heartbeat signal from Pine Gap.
Ping... (2 seconds silence)... ping... (2 seconds)... ping...
Then a massive sub-bass impact on the title card.
Resonance holds for 3 seconds, then fades to absolute silence.

Instruments: synthesized radar/sonar ping (sine wave, 1kHz,
short decay), sub-bass impact (30Hz, long decay).

Mood: finality. The weight of what you've just witnessed settles.
The story is bigger than you thought.
```

### 場景配樂模板 (Scene-Specific Music Templates)

#### 台灣戰場 — 孤立與決心 (Taiwan: Isolation & Resolve)
```
Ambient military tension with subtle Asian instrumentation accents.
Erhu or guzheng — single sustained notes, textural not melodic.
Deep taiko hits on scene transitions. Electronic pulse underneath
like a failing heartbeat monitor.
Reference: Ryuichi Sakamoto "Merry Christmas Mr Lawrence" meets
Hildur Gudnadottir "Joker" — intimate despair within vast events.
Tempo: 70-80 BPM. Key: E minor.
Mood: a small island standing alone against an ocean of force.
```

#### 歐洲戰場 — 寒冷與游擊 (Europe: Cold & Guerrilla)
```
Cold orchestral, Eastern European folk undertones.
Solo cello or violin in minor key, sparse arrangement.
Distant artillery booms as rhythmic element (not sampled —
composed to feel like artillery cadence).
Accordion drone underneath (not cheerful — mournful).
Reference: Mica Levi "Under the Skin" meets Alexandre Desplat
"The Imitation Game" — intellectual dread in frozen landscapes.
Tempo: 60-70 BPM. Key: C minor.
Mood: the cold is an enemy too. Survival is quiet and patient.
```

#### 中東戰場 — 沙漠與虛無 (Middle East: Desert & Nihilism)
```
Minimalist, heat-shimmer atmosphere. Vast empty space in the mix.
Oud or duduk — single sustained note, huge reverb tail.
Sand/wind texture as constant bed, low drone (50-80Hz),
occasional metallic percussion (finger cymbals, distant).
Reference: Jonny Greenwood "There Will Be Blood" meets
Carter Burwell "No Country for Old Men" — violence as weather.
Tempo: free time / rubato. No steady pulse.
Mood: Kane's nihilism made sonic. Nothing matters except the
next 100 meters.
```

#### 諜報/背叛場景 (Espionage/Betrayal Scenes)
```
Minimal electronic, tense and claustrophobic.
Single repeating synth motif (2-3 notes), detuned slightly.
Ticking clock or dripping water as metronomic element.
Low string drone underneath, barely audible.
Reference: Trent Reznor "Gone Girl" meets Alexandre Desplat
"Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy" — paranoia as music.
Tempo: 100 BPM but sparse (feels slower than it is).
Mood: the walls are listening. Trust no one.
```

#### 決戰/反攻場景 (Counter-Attack / Final Battle)
```
Full military orchestral with electronic enhancement.
Brass fanfare (not heroic — desperate), driving percussion
(snare rolls, bass drum impacts), string section ostinato.
Electric guitar power chords on key hits.
Reference: Hans Zimmer "The Dark Knight" meets Ludwig Goransson
"Tenet" — mechanical, relentless forward momentum.
Tempo: 140+ BPM. Key: D minor → D major at climax (earned).
Mood: this is not glory. This is survival by force of will.
The major key resolution should feel like exhaustion, not celebration.
```

## 三、音效 (SFX)

### 軍事音效清單 (Military SFX Prompts)

For ElevenLabs Sound Effects, Freesound.org, or dedicated SFX generators:

#### 雷達與通訊 (Radar & Communications)
```
- Radar sweep: rhythmic electronic ping with CRT crackle, 3-second
  rotation cycle, faint hum between sweeps
- Radio static: white noise with squelch, broken voice fragments in
  Mandarin cutting through, rapid tuning between frequencies
- Link-16 data burst: rapid digital chirp sequence (sounds like a
  fax machine crossed with Morse code), 0.5 second bursts
- Satellite failure: descending tone from high frequency to sub-bass,
  signal dropout with digital artifacts, silence
- System boot: ascending electronic tone sequence, fan spin-up,
  CRT power-on crackle, cursor blinking sound
- Heartbeat signal (Pine Gap): clean electronic ping, 1kHz sine wave,
  300ms duration, 2-second interval, slight reverb — like a heart
  monitor in an empty room
- Encrypted transmission: garbled digital noise, rhythmic but
  unintelligible, sounds like underwater speech
```

#### 武器與爆炸 (Weapons & Explosions)
```
- Anti-radiation missile impact: rising roar (3 seconds) → sharp
  ignition crack → massive concussive explosion with echo off
  mountain walls, debris falling for 5+ seconds after
- Tank main gun (120mm): massive concussive boom with metallic ring
  and pressure wave, ground-shaking low-frequency thump, shell
  casing ejection clatter
- XM7 rifle fire (6.8mm): sharp supersonic crack, heavier than 5.56,
  distinctive metallic action cycling, 2-3 round bursts
- Fighter jet flyby (F-16V): Doppler-shifted turbine whine,
  approaching from left → center → right, afterburner rumble,
  sonic envelope of pressure change
- Small arms firefight (distant): staccato cracks at varied distances
  (100m, 300m, 500m+), echoing off buildings/mountains, no
  Hollywood rhythmic patterns — random, overlapping, chaotic
- Drone swarm: high-pitched multi-motor buzz, hundreds of small
  electric motors creating an insect-like swarm sound,
  approaching from distance, ominous
- Artillery barrage (distant): deep rolling thunder, 5-10 second
  intervals, ground vibration, not crisp — muffled by distance
```

#### 載具 (Vehicles)
```
- Leopard 2A8 engine idle: deep diesel rumble with turbocharger whine,
  metallic track clinking, hydraulic system hum
- Military helicopter (UH-60): deep rotor thump (not civilian Huey
  sound — deeper, more mechanical), turbine whine, wind buffeting
- Naval vessel (carrier): constant deep hum of engines, wind across
  flight deck, distant catapult launch steam release
```

```
5. Glass breaking far away (2-3 blocks)

8. Military helicopter rotor (deep thrum, passing overhead)
```

```
6. Ice cracking on branches (sharp, sudden)
```

#### 中東沙漠 (Middle East Desert)
```
Layers:
1. Wind — constant, low, carrying fine sand particles (granular texture)
2. Heat shimmer hum (very low frequency drone, 40-60Hz,
   barely perceptible — more felt than heard)
3. Distant explosion reverberating across open terrain
   (long decay time, 5+ seconds of echo)
4. Vehicle engine struggling through sand
5. Metallic clink of equipment and ammunition on tactical gear
6. Silence — vast, oppressive, tinnitus-inducing (after removing
   all other layers, what remains is the sound of empty space)
7. Flies buzzing (intermittent, implying heat and death)
8. Sand hitting against metal or fabric (constant, textural)

```

#### 潛艦/海戰 (Submarine / Naval)
```
Layers:
1. Deep ocean pressure hum (very low, constant)
2. Sonar pings (varying distances, some own-ship, some contacts)
3. Hull creaking under pressure (occasional, metallic groaning)
4. Machinery vibration (constant low thrum)
5. Water rushing past hull (muffled)
6. Torpedo launch: compressed air burst, propeller whine departing
7. Depth charge / distant underwater explosion: muffled thud followed
   by pressure wave rattle through hull

Mood: claustrophobic, pressurized, blind. Hearing is the only
sense that works.
```

## 音訊製作參考 (Audio Production Notes)

### Mixing Guidelines
- Voiceover should sit at -6dB, music at -18dB under VO, SFX at -12dB
- Use sidechain compression: duck music when VO is present
- All military radio dialogue should have bandpass filter (300Hz-3kHz)
  to simulate radio transmission
- Add subtle room reverb to bunker scenes, zero reverb to desert scenes
- The Pine Gap heartbeat ping should be the cleanest, most isolated
  sound in the entire production — it represents hope cutting through noise

### File Format Standards
- Voiceover: WAV 48kHz/24bit, mono
- Music: WAV 48kHz/24bit, stereo
- SFX: WAV 48kHz/24bit, mono (stereo for ambients)
- Final mix: WAV 48kHz/24bit, stereo → export MP4 AAC 320kbps for delivery
