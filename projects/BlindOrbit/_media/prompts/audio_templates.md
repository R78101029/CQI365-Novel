## 二、配樂 (Music — Suno / Udio)

### 預告片配樂結構 (Trailer Score Structure)

#### Phase 1: Peace (0:00-0:15)
```
Style: ambient orchestral, single piano note with reverb,
distant string pad, very sparse. Like Ryuichi Sakamoto meets
Max Richter's "On the Nature of Daylight".
Tempo: 60 BPM. Key: D minor.
Instruments: solo piano, ambient pads, distant cello.
Mood: beautiful but fragile, like glass about to shatter.
```

#### Phase 2: The Strike (0:15-0:37)
```
Style: electronic pulse mixed with orchestral tension.
Hans Zimmer "Dunkirk" ticking clock motif.
Tempo: builds from 80 to 110 BPM.
Instruments: Shepard tone (ever-rising), staccato strings,
deep sub-bass pulses, glitchy digital artifacts,
distorted radio static samples.
Mood: systemic collapse, technological dread.
Build: gradual crescendo with increasing rhythmic density.
```

#### Phase 3: Chaos (0:37-0:59)
```
Style: full orchestral assault meets industrial electronic.
Reference: Trent Reznor / Atticus Ross "The Social Network"
meets Junkie XL "Mad Max Fury Road".
Tempo: 130 BPM, driving.
Instruments: brass section hits, taiko drums, distorted guitars,
synthetic bass drops, rapid-fire percussion.
Mood: three simultaneous wars — overwhelming, kinetic, visceral.
```

#### Phase 4: Betrayal (0:59-1:07)
```
Style: sudden silence, then single dissonant chord.
Reference: the "BRAAAM" from Inception but more subtle.
Instruments: low brass, reverse cymbal, heartbeat thump.
Mood: the knife twist — realization of internal betrayal.
Drop all instruments to near silence, just a pulse.
```

#### Phase 5: Hope (1:07-1:22)
```
Style: rebuilt from the silence. Piano returns with strings.
Reference: Hans Zimmer "Interstellar" organ meets
Jóhann Jóhannsson "Arrival".
Tempo: 90 BPM, steady and purposeful.
Instruments: piano melody (same as Phase 1 but transposed up),
building strings, French horn, military snare roll.
Mood: desperate determination, not triumph — earned hope.
```

#### Phase 6: Title (1:22-1:30)
```
Style: all instruments cut. Single electronic ping —
the heartbeat signal from Pine Gap. Ping... ping... ping...
Then silence. Bass drop into title card.
Instruments: synthesized radar ping, sub-bass impact.
Mood: finality, the weight of what you've just witnessed.
```

### 場景配樂模板 (Scene Music Templates)

#### 台灣戰場 — 孤立與決心
```
Ambient military tension, Asian instrumentation accents.
Erhu or guzheng melody (single note, not melodic — textural).
Deep taiko hits on transitions. Electronic pulse underneath.
Reference: Ryuichi Sakamoto "Merry Christmas Mr Lawrence"
meets Hildur Guðnadóttir "Joker".
```

#### 歐洲戰場 — 寒冷與游擊
```
Cold orchestral, Eastern European folk undertones.
Solo cello or violin in minor key, sparse arrangement.
Distant artillery booms as rhythmic element.
Reference: Mica Levi "Under the Skin" meets
Alexandre Desplat "The Imitation Game".
```

#### 中東戰場 — 沙漠與虛無
```
Minimalist, heat-shimmer atmosphere.
Oud or duduk (single sustained note), vast reverb.
Sand/wind texture, low drone, occasional metallic percussion.
Reference: Jonny Greenwood "There Will Be Blood" meets
Carter Burwell "No Country for Old Men".
```

## 三、音效 (SFX)

### 軍事音效清單 (Military SFX)

```
- Radar sweep: rhythmic electronic ping with CRT crackle
- Radio static: white noise, squelch, broken voice fragments
- Link-16 data burst: rapid digital chirp sequence
- Satellite failure: descending tone, signal dropout
- Missile launch: rising roar with sharp ignition crack
- Tank main gun: massive concussive boom with metallic ring
- Fighter jet flyby: Doppler-shifted turbine whine
- Small arms firefight: staccato cracks, varied distances
- Emergency alarm: klaxon horn, pulsing, industrial
- Heartbeat signal (Pine Gap): slow electronic ping, medical monitor quality
```
